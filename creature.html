ABOUT THE PROJECT

Designed and modeled for a horror game prototype, from concept to rigged model with custom animations.

Modeled from scratch, details baked onto 15k polys of manual retopology mesh.

Vertex weights are painted to match the distorted form of the muscles, allowing for realistic deformations during animations.
