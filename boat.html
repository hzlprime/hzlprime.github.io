ABOUT THE PROJECT

A mini game where you play as a boat to collect as many plastic ducks as you can, in a minute timer.

The steering and speeding of the boat is mapped with MIDI inputs through a custom remote controller device, created with a Teensy.

This game was made in three days, for the TU3B Gesture Capture - Hardware Control Assignment, plus an additional day for the "making of" video.

Music by AudioCoffee: www.audiocoffee.net
