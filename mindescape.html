ABOUT THE PROJECT

An exploration of human-object perception through the meta-mind of a wall, one of the most fundamental elements of space-making.

Blending generative computational design, interactive videomapping, and immersive technologies like AR and VR to create an experience that bridges the gap between physical and digital realities.
